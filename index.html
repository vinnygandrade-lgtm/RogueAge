Expedition Rules

Loot and combat XP stay in your Expedition Bag until you extract. Pick a path each journey — extract for 100%, death keeps half.

🎒
Expedition Bag
Adena, drops, and combat XP go into the bag — not your inventory or character — until you extract or die.
💀
If you die
You keep half of the bag (Adena, items, and XP). The other half is lost forever.
🏃
Extract
On the map, use Collect & exit to keep 100% of the bag. Boss wins do not auto-extract — cash out yourself. In combat there is no retreat — you cannot leave until you win or die.
🗺️
Roguelike loop
Journey 1 is fight-only. From journey 2: safe paths, merchants, and gifts. Boss gate every 10 journeys. Taking a safe path can force the next journey to be combat-only.
⚡
Run builds
Upgrade cards fill build paths. Unlock as many as you can — bonuses stack. Mastery rewards at 3, 5, and 7 builds. Track them on the Builds tab.
🎯
Deals & rare events
Merchant and Warhorn open a pick (deals or rally packages; paid deals spend bag Adena). Rare events — shrine, gambler, cache, storm — also ask you to choose.
🔭
Scout & Tracks
Scout biases the next journey toward fight-heavy or safer paths. Tracks can guarantee a path type appears. Watch the map banners before you pick.
Path cards
  • ⚔️ Fight — win to pick a run upgrade
  • 👹 Boss — brutal fight, ×2 bag loot
  • 💀 Elite — champions, premium loot
  • 🎁 Chest — bag loot (from journey 2)
  • 🧳 Merchant — pick 1 of 3 deals (bag Adena)
  • 🔨 Forge — temporary +1 gear enchant for the run
  • 🔭 Scout — foresight for next journey · bag bonus
  • 🚶 Patrol — safe XP march (early journeys)
  • 👣 Tracks — next trait · chance to force a path
  • 📯 Warhorn — pick Assault, Tempo, or Iron Rally
  • 🌿 Ambush — bag loot or HP loss
